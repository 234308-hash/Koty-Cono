BEFORE YOUR FIRST BITE

A few little answers.

What makes Koty Cono different?+

Our concept puts low-fat ice cream at the center, with playful flavors and creamy textures. Final nutrition and ingredients will be listed on the product packaging before live sales.

Can I customize my cone?+

Absolutely. Open a flavor to choose a regular or large size and pick an extra topping. Your choices travel with the item in your bag.

Which payment methods are available?+

This store currently runs a clearly labeled demo checkout. You can preview cash on delivery, cards and mobile wallets. No payment is collected and no delivery is arranged. Online payments will be available after the brand's payment account is connected.

How does delivery work?+

The demo calculates a delivery fee by city and gives free delivery on baskets of EGP 350 or more. Live delivery areas and timings will be confirmed before real ordering begins.

What about allergens?+

Our sample dairy flavors contain milk; waffle cones contain wheat. Pistachio and nut toppings contain nuts. Check each product's ingredient and allergen information. Production cross-contact information must be confirmed for the final menu.

Can I save my favorites?+

Tap a heart on any flavor. Your saved flavors and bag stay on this browser, so you can come back to your happy little shortlist.
